About Innovatech

EMPOWERING THE SAHARA WITH CUTTING-EDGE AI

Born within the halls of the Centre Universitaire d'Illizi (CU Illizi), INNOVATECH is a dynamic student-led scientific and technological club dedicated to transforming theory into practical engineering.

From deploying computer vision AI models that make desert roads safer to building smart autonomous rovers and introducing primary school children in Illizi to Virtual Reality, we believe technology should solve regional challenges and spark wonder in every child.

Our Mission

Equip university students with modern AI, programming, and hardware engineering competencies while actively contributing to local societal solutions.

Our Vision: "We Are The Future"

Position CU Illizi as a thriving hub of artificial intelligence and robotics excellence in southern Algeria.

Flagship Innovations

SOLVING SAHARAN CHALLENGES WITH AI

Directly featured in our scientific posters at CU Illizi: real algorithms solving critical regional problems.

Computer Vision • YOLO Road Safety Project

CAMEL DETECTION AI SYSTEM

Vehicle-camel collisions remain a major danger along Saharan highways in Algeria (RN3 / Illizi region). Our team engineered a real-time object detection architecture using YOLO (You Only Look Once) trained on arid desert road imagery to detect crossing camels at long range and instantly trigger driver collision alerts.

Architecture YOLOv8 Ultralytics
Hardware Embedded Jetson / Pi
Tested with CU Illizi Research Faculty
EcoTech & IoT • YOLOv8 Circular Economy

SMART REVERSE VENDING MACHINE (RVM)

To combat desert plastic waste, we proposed an automated reverse vending machine (RVM). Using an onboard camera with YOLOv8 bottle detection and barcode matching, the smart kiosk validates plastic bottles deposited by users and provides instant rewards via a companion mobile app.

Detection Logic Real-time Bottle AI
User Incentives Mobile Rewards App
